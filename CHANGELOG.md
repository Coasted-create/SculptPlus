# Changelog

The section for a version becomes the text of its release.

## 0.2.2

- The other side now mirrors through the middle of the face or body itself, however the head is held (with a tilted head the two eyes came out different)
- Sliders, the dice and the arrows on the part you hold go by the body's own side, up and front, not by the pose of the moment

## 0.2.1

- A part's dot, glow and arrows sit on the skin the part moves instead of on the point it turns around: the eye parts no longer pile up in the middle of the eye
- The soft brush measures how far apart parts are by that skin, too
- The soft brush no longer takes along parts that sit in the very same spot (with a brush on, every eye part moved as one)

## 0.2.0

- Parts light up under the mouse instead of showing a dot each (dots stay as a choice); Ctrl + wheel goes through parts that lie on top of each other
- A map of the face and body: click an area to work on it
- Sliders for the area you picked: eye shape, lids, brows, nose, lips, cheekbones, chin and more
- Focus camera: front, three-quarter and side views, zoom far closer than the game allows, the picked area in the middle; "Hide my hat"
- Fine work: Ctrl+drag is ten times finer, tiny steps with arrows or Alt + arrow keys
- Workbench: a window with your own head as a clay model. Turn it freely, zoom as close as you like, and take hold of the skin; the area a part moves lights up

## 0.1.5

- Hide the dot of a single part ("Hide its dot" under the part you hold, or Alt+H); "Show them again" brings them back

## 0.1.4

- Dots no longer come and go as you turn the camera: the far side of the face is told by direction, and a dot that would lie on another one is put next to it

## 0.1.3

- Stop asks first when the sculpt is not kept as a shape yet
- "Go on with my last sculpt" on the start page brings back what you stopped or closed, kept or not
- Parts that sit in the same spot as another one (the iris in the eye) get their own dot next to it

## 0.1.2

- A Freeze button in the top row (also Alt+F, also in the small window)
- Freezing now stops every animation layer (body, face, lips), not only the overall speed
- The Movement slider is the slow motion for when you are not frozen

## 0.1.1

- Arrows and rings on the part you hold: move, size or turn it along one direction only
- A/B: hold a button or Alt+B to compare the sculpt with a kept shape
- A title bar button that shrinks the window to its top row (the dots keep working)
- Title bar buttons no longer sit on top of the close button
- Freezing the character while sculpting now holds

## 0.1.0

First public build.
